💸 RupeeLens — Financial Visibility & Intelligence

🔍 See Beyond Spending. Understand Your Money.

RupeeLens is a student-built financial visibility dashboard designed to help people understand their spending habits, identify potential expense leaks, track subscriptions, and make smarter savings decisions.

Our goal is to make personal finance easier to understand—even for people with little or no financial knowledge.

🌐 Live Website: https://willowy-tiramisu-090a1a.netlify.app/

---

🎯 Problem Statement

Many people struggle to understand where their money goes each month. Small recurring expenses, unnecessary spending, forgotten subscriptions, and unexpected charges can affect their savings.

Traditional expense tracking often focuses on recording transactions. RupeeLens aims to go a step further by presenting financial information through visual summaries, spending insights, forecasts, and savings simulations.

💡 Our Solution

RupeeLens provides a single dashboard where users can record transactions, understand their spending patterns, explore potential expense leaks, and plan their savings.

The interface is designed to be simple, visual, and beginner-friendly so that users can understand their finances without needing advanced financial knowledge.

✨ Key Features

📊 1. Financial Overview

- View total income and expenses.
- Calculate the remaining balance.
- Monitor the number of potential expense leaks.
- Understand your financial position at a glance.

💳 2. Transaction Management

- Record income and expenses.
- Enter transaction descriptions, amounts, dates, and categories.
- Classify spending to understand financial habits.
- Review recorded transactions.

🚨 3. Expense Leak Radar

- Explore spending patterns.
- Identify recurring costs and potential unnecessary expenses.
- Help users become more aware of where their money goes.

🔄 4. Subscription Manager

- Add subscriptions and their payment amounts.
- Track billing frequency and upcoming billing dates.
- Estimate annual subscription costs.
- Understand the long-term impact of recurring payments.

📅 5. 12-Month Money Forecast

- Create monthly expense plans.
- Compare planned expenses with actual spending.
- Review estimated expenses across different months.
- Support better budgeting and financial planning.

💰 6. What-If Savings Simulator

- Experiment with reducing flexible spending.
- Estimate potential monthly savings.
- Calculate potential annual savings.
- Explore how small spending changes could affect savings.

🕵️ 7. Money Leak Detective

- Present a leak score to help users assess potential expense leaks.
- Highlight spending patterns that may deserve further investigation.
- Encourage users to review their financial habits.

⚠️ 8. Financial Anomaly Radar

- Aim to identify unusual spending deviations.
- Highlight potential duplicate charges.
- Flag sudden increases in merchant spending for review.

These indicators are intended to support financial awareness, not to guarantee fraud detection.

🏆 9. Smart Savings Challenge

- Encourage users to work towards savings goals.
- Display savings challenge progress.
- Use badges and achievements to make financial discipline more engaging.

🤖 10. Rupees Lens Money AI

- Provide a conversational interface for money-related questions.
- Aim to make financial information easier to understand.
- Support questions about expenses, spending habits, and remaining balances.

The actual capabilities of the Money AI depend on the functionality implemented in the project.

🌟 What Makes RupeeLens Different?

- Beginner-friendly: Designed for people without advanced financial knowledge.
- More than transaction recording: Combines financial summaries, expense awareness, forecasting, and savings simulations.
- Expense leak awareness: Encourages users to investigate recurring and potentially unnecessary spending.
- What-if planning: Helps users explore possible savings before changing their habits.
- Visual and interactive: Brings multiple personal finance tools together in one dashboard.
- Goal-oriented: Uses savings challenges to encourage better financial habits.

Our focus is to turn financial data into information that people can understand and act on.

🖥️ How to Run the Project Locally

You can run RupeeLens on your computer without installing a backend server.

1. Clone this repository:
   
   git clone YOUR_GITHUB_REPOSITORY_URL

2. Open the downloaded project folder in Visual Studio Code.

3. Locate "index.html".

4. Open "index.html" in your browser.

5. Add sample transactions and explore the available dashboard features.

Note: Replace "YOUR_GITHUB_REPOSITORY_URL" with the HTTPS URL of your actual GitHub repository.

🔒 Privacy and Limitations

- Users should avoid entering sensitive banking credentials or confidential financial information.
- Financial insights depend on the information entered by the user and the functionality implemented.
- Potential expense leaks and anomalies should be reviewed before taking action.
- Forecasts and savings simulations are estimates, not guarantees.
- The project should not be treated as a substitute for professional financial advice.
- Features such as live bank integration, verified fraud detection, and externally connected AI should not be assumed unless separately implemented and tested.
