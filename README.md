RupeeLens 💰

Financial Visibility Dashboard

About the Project

RupeeLens is a financial visibility dashboard that helps users understand their spending habits and identify potential expense leaks.

Our goal is to make financial awareness simple and accessible, even for people with limited financial knowledge.

Problem Statement

Many people find it difficult to understand where their money goes. Small, frequent expenses can accumulate and affect their ability to manage money effectively.

Our Solution

RupeeLens presents financial information through a user-friendly dashboard, helping users review expenses, recognise spending patterns, and identify potential savings opportunities.

Key Features

- Expense tracking
- Financial dashboard
- Spending visualisations
- Potential expense leak identification
- User-friendly interface
